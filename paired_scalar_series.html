Known Stuff#

Fixed-boundary source#

The fixed-boundary theorem supplies four semantic roles: rule and deletion actions for each letter in {b, c}. A role word is accepted exactly when its upper concatenation followed by the fixed marker equals its lower concatenation.

U(w)M = V(w).

For the emitted restricted tag systems, a nonempty accepted role word exists exactly when the tag computation halts.

Word-pair matrices#

Encode letters by nonzero ternary digits. The standard word-pair representation assigns each pair (u, v) a 3 × 3 matrix Ψ(u, v). It preserves concatenation, and one fixed row and column detect u = v.

A unimodular basis change separates the upper and lower word channels:

Φ(u,v) = [ 1 σ(v) σ(u) 0 3|v| 0 0 0 3|u| ].

For a fixed letter, its rule and deletion roles have the same upper word. Their matrices therefore agree on the two-dimensional upper-channel plane.

New Stuff#

Result#

Paired scalar series. For every admissible deletion width β and tag body B, there are three explicit 4 × 4 integer matrices indexed by two data letters and one toggle, together with an integer row r and column c, such that

pβ,B (z) = rT Gzc,

and pβ,B(z) = 0 exactly when the decoded role word satisfies the fixed-boundary equation. The equivalence holds for every raw control word, including arbitrary toggles.

Phase quotient#

Start with two copies of the three-dimensional word-pair space, one for each phase. Let E be the two-dimensional agreement plane. The anti-diagonal copy

K={(x,−x):x∈E}

is killed by both data controls and preserved by the phase toggle. All three maps descend to the quotient

W=(V⊕V)/K, dimW=2·3−2=4.

The boundary row annihilates K, so the scalar coefficient survives the quotient exactly.

Complete decoder#

Matrix products act on the boundary column from right to left. The decoder therefore scans a control word from its suffix. It begins in rule phase; a toggle exchanges phases; a data letter emits its rule or deletion role according to the current phase and then enters deletion phase.

Induction gives coefficient preservation for every control word. A second induction inserts at most one toggle before each encoded suffix and proves that every role word has a control spelling. Hence the raw three-letter series and the four-role fixed-boundary series have the same nonempty zero language.

Exact dimension#

Four explicit prefixes and four explicit suffixes form a 4 × 4 finite Hankel section with nonzero determinant for every admissible source. Any exact linear representation of this scalar series over ℚ therefore has at least four states. The quotient construction has four states, so its realization dimension is exact.

Dependents#

  • Paired-role compression uses the four-state matrices directly and adds a rank-one separator.
  • The shared-partial compiler binary-encodes the three controls while retaining a common unfinished word channel.
  • The tilted separator changes nonzero coefficients without changing this series’ zero language, enabling a nine-dimensional return realization.
