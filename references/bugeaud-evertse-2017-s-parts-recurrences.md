## Synopsis

For a fixed finite set `S` of primes, the paper bounds the `S`-part of terms of a nondegenerate
integer linear recurrence sequence. In the general asymptotic theorem, the `S`-part is at most
`|uₙ|^ε` for all sufficiently large `n`; this uses the Subspace Theorem and is ineffective.

The authors also give weaker but effective power-saving estimates under additional hypotheses,
notably the existence of a dominant characteristic root or the binary-recurrence setting. The
nondegeneracy condition excludes quotients of distinct characteristic roots that are roots of
unity, and the recurrence itself is fixed while the index varies.

The theorems do not cover a sequence obtained by changing the recurrence matrix at each index,
even if every individual branch is linear. A reduction to one fixed recurrence, or to finitely
many fixed subsequences, is required before the estimates apply.
