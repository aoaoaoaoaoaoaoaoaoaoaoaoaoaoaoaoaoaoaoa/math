## Synopsis

The paper develops convergence criteria for p-adic continued fractions in terms of the
valuations of their partial quotients. It compares several classical algorithms, identifies
digit conditions sufficient for convergence, and constructs a three-step algorithm in which
the valuation pattern is controlled across each block.

For rational inputs, the three-step algorithm terminates. The proof uses a strict descent of an
integer numerator-denominator measure over each bounded block; allowing one adverse digit does
not defeat the blockwise decrease. The paper also studies approximation quality and periodicity
phenomena for quadratic inputs.

The termination theorem depends on the algorithm's prescribed three-phase digit rule. It does
not imply that every convergent p-adic continued fraction of a rational number terminates, nor
does it apply to an unbounded-state choice rule without a macro-step reduction.
