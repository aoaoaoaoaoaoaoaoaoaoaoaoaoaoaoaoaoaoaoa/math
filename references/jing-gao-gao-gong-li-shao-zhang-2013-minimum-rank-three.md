## Results used

The paper relates rank-three sign patterns to point-line configurations in the projective
plane and exhibits field dependence between real and rational minimum rank. It supplies
geometric language for finite rank-three zero-pattern experiments.

For a same-zero `3 × 3` compiler this is only a finite incidence model. The actual
obligation is stronger: the rows and columns of every Hankel section must be generated by
three common shift matrices. Minimum rank without this shift equivariance cannot establish
a rational-series realization.

## Audit notes

The local arXiv preprint predates the expanded journal article “Minimum ranks of sign
patterns and zero–nonzero patterns and point–hyperplane configurations,” *Linear Algebra
and its Applications* **558** (2018), 44–62,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2018.08.019. The journal version adds Wei Fang and treats
zero–nonzero patterns explicitly. We do not import its stronger statements into the present
record.
