## Results used

The paper proves the golden-ratio abundance bound for exact families and its robust analogue
for approximately union-closed families. More importantly for strategy selection, it constructs
approximate families showing that `(3 − √5)/2` is the sharp endpoint for the independent-
sample entropy mechanism under approximate closure.

## Audit notes

This is a barrier for the independent-coupling route, not for exact union closure or dependent
couplings. No redistributable version was located, so the reviewed PDF is not retained.
