## Synopsis

The paper defines a broad class of P-adic continued-fraction algorithms over a number field
`K`. A type `τ=(K,P,s)` consists of a distinguished non-Archimedean place and a locally constant
floor function taking values integral away from that place. Continued-fraction finiteness (CFF)
means that every element of `K` terminates; continued-fraction periodicity (CFP) allows a finite
or periodic expansion.

Its principal height criterion assigns to a type a quantity `ντ` formed from the product of
Archimedean continuant norms of each admissible partial quotient divided by its expansion at the
distinguished place. If `ντ≤1`, complete quotients have bounded Weil height and Northcott's
theorem gives CFP. If `ντ<1`, the resulting strict height decay gives CFF. The proof records the
explicit inequality `H(αₙ₊₁)ᵈ ≤ C ντⁿ H(α)ᵈ`, which isolates exactly where strictness is needed.

The later sections construct types satisfying the criterion for norm-Euclidean fields and for
prime ideals in norm-Euclidean ideal classes, and derive class-group obstructions to the
existence of CFF types. The paper does not claim that every locally defined P-adic algorithm
fits a type or satisfies the criterion; a single scalar Gauss recurrence and a genuine floor
function are part of the hypotheses.
