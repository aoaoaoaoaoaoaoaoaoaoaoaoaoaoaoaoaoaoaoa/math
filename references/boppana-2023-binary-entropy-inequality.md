## Results used

Boppana gives a short analytic proof of the binary-entropy inequality needed for the
`(3 − √5)/2` bound. It supplies an independent, non-numerical audit of the scalar core of
Alweiss–Huang–Sellke and fixes the baseline without depending on their tabulated check.

## Audit notes

The result certifies the independent-coupling endpoint only. It neither analyzes Sawin’s
dependent coupling nor supports the numerical constants `0.38234` or `0.38271`.
