## Synopsis

The paper constructs continued-fraction algorithms for the commensurability classes of
noncocompact triangle groups with quadratic invariant trace field and proves termination on
the base field. Its proof studies the action of positive unimodular matrices on absolute Weil
height. Theorem 4.3 gives an explicit real interval on which a positive matrix is
height-increasing; the threshold incorporates the size of the conjugate matrix and also
describes equality and height-decreasing loci.

Definition 5.2 packages finite symbolic words into decreasing blocks. Theorem 5.3 supplies a
complete set of such blocks for the algorithms constructed in the paper, so every nonparabolic
symbolic path has strict height descent at successive block ends. Northcott finiteness then
forces the orbit into a parabolic endpoint.

Over `ℚ`, the relevant observation is elementary and stronger: a positive matrix in
`PSL₂±(ℤ)` strictly raises the primitive numerator-denominator height of every positive rational.
Consequently an inverse branch defined by such a matrix strictly lowers height. The quadratic
results require specially constructed interval partitions and complete block sets; they do not
assert descent for an arbitrary piecewise-projective system.

The Smith decoder is already a positive integral determinant-`−1` matrix. This paper isolates
the exact remaining route to an elementary rational descent: orient a legal guard macro as the
inverse decoder on one positive primitive cone, and prove that the moving p-power gauge either
preserves that cone-and-height calculation or belongs to a finite complete block family.
