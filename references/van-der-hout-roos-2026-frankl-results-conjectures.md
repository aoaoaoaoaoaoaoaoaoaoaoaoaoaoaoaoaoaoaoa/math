## Results used

The paper confirms explicitly that Frankl’s conjecture remained open in April 2026. It defines
a tight family as one in which every frequency is at most one half and proves that the assertion
“every tight union-closed family has even cardinality” is equivalent to Frankl. Stronger
computationally motivated conjectures say that every clean tight family is a power set.

## Audit notes

The equivalence is elementary and valid. The stronger complement-closure and power-set claims
are based on mixed-integer experiments for universes through seven elements; they are conjectures,
not structural theorems. The article contributes no new universal abundance constant.
