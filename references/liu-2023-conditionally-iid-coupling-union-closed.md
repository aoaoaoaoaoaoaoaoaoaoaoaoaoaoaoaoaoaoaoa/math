## Results used

Liu proves analytically that conditionally IID couplings strictly improve the best constant
obtainable from Sawin’s two-coupling mixture. A stronger finite-dimensional program suggests
`c′=0.382709087918741`, characterized by explicit scalar equations.

## Audit notes

The numerical value `c′` is conditional on two unproved statements: positive semidefiniteness of
an infinite coefficient matrix for the kernel generated by `f(x)=x(1−x)`, and a particular global
optimizer shape for a nine-dimensional nonconvex program. The paper verifies finite truncations
and grids for the first and reports `10^5` random-start Matlab runs for the second. The conference
publication makes the analytic strict-improvement mechanism peer reviewed, but does not turn
`0.382709…` into an unconditional certified constant.
