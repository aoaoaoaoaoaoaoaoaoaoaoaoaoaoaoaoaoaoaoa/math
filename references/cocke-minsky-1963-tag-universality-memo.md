## Result used

The paper proves universality with deletion width two. Its alphabet is not fixed:
Section 4 assigns a family of tag symbols to every state of the simulated Turing
machine. The theorem therefore fixes deletion width while allowing the alphabet to
grow; it does not supply binary fixed-width universality.

## Audit notes

The local file is the MIT memo deposited by MIT Libraries, not the typeset JACM
version. The mathematical construction is the published construction. The paper
uses `P` for deletion width.
