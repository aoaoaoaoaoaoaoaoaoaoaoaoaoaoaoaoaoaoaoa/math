## Synopsis

The paper decides vector reachability, point-to-point reachability by fractional-linear maps,
and scalar reachability for finitely generated semigroups in `SL₂(ℤ)`. Its central device is an
effective description of every matrix carrying one primitive vector or projective point to
another. After encoding `SL₂(ℤ)` through canonical modular-group words, those solution sets
become regular languages.

Theorem 14 decides vector reachability and fractional-linear point reachability. Proposition 15
retains decidability under an additional regular constraint on the generator word. Theorem 16
decides scalar reachability `xᵀMy=λ` by decomposing the target affine line into finitely many
regularly describable vector-reachability families.

All matrices have determinant one. The proofs do not cover arbitrary `GL₂(ℚ)`, determinant
growth, singular factors, or a countably parametrized family that has not first been represented
by a finite automaton over `SL₂(ℤ)`.
