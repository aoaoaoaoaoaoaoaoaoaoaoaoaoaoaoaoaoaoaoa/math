## Synopsis

The paper applies a moving form of Schmidt's Subspace Theorem to greatest common divisors of
multivariable polynomials evaluated at moving S-unit points. The polynomial degrees remain
fixed, the two polynomials are coprime, at least one has a nonzero constant term, and the heights
of their moving coefficients must be negligible relative to the height of the S-unit point.

Under those hypotheses, either the logarithmic gcd is arbitrarily small relative to point
height on an infinite subsequence, or the S-unit points lie in a finite union of moving
translates of proper algebraic subgroups. The latter alternative is an explicit multiplicative
degeneracy, not an uncontrolled exceptional set. Applications treat gcds of pairs of algebraic
linear recurrence sequences.

The result is ineffective and its small-gcd alternative is guaranteed only on an infinite
subset in the stated moving form. It requires a fixed finite `S` and fixed ambient torus rank;
arbitrary new prime support or coefficient height comparable to orbit height invalidates the
criterion.
