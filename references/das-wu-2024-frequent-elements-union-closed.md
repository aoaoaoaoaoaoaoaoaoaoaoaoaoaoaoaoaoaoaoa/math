## Results used

Das and Wu settle Nagel’s fixed-`k` frequency conjecture for every `k≥2` and prove an
asymptotic golden-ratio lower bound for the `k`th-most frequent element. Their July 2025
version describes the general abundance frontier as approximately `0.3823455`, while citing
Liu; this corroborates that the conditional `0.382709…` value was not accepted as an
unconditional theorem.

## Audit notes

The paper concerns multiple frequent elements and does not improve the first-element universal
constant. Its status discussion is corroborative only; the exact obligations are taken from Yu,
Cambie, and Liu themselves.
