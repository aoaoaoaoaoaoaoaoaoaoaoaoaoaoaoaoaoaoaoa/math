## Synopsis

For fixed numbers `m,n≥2`, the paper encodes an ordered list of `m` distinct subsets of
`[n]` by Boolean variables and constructs a Boolean polynomial `ICC_{m,n}`. Its factors
test pairwise distinctness, intersection closure, and failure of every coordinate to occur
in at most half of the sets. Theorem 1 states that Frankl’s intersection-closed formulation
holds for `(m,n)` exactly when this polynomial is the zero Boolean function.

The paper cites Lozin and Zamaraev only to distinguish its Boolean-polynomial encoding
from their Horn-function formulation. It does not discuss bidual Horn functions, Karpas’s
half-cube theorem, or a density dichotomy between true and false points.

## Source Assessment

The central theorem is a direct characteristic-function encoding, not a new verified class
or abundance bound. The source contains minor typographical defects, including “zoo” for
“zero” in Theorem 1, but they do not obscure the equivalence. Semantic Scholar identified
this paper as the sole forward citation of Lozin and Zamaraev located on 2026-08-08; its full
text therefore closes that branch of the bidual-Horn priority check.
