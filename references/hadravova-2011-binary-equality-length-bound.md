# Hadravová (2011)

**Citation.** Jana Hadravová, “A length bound for binary equality words,”
*Commentationes Mathematicae Universitatis Carolinae* **52**(1), 1–20, 2011.

- Canonical record: https://dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/141422
- Local PDF: none
- Retrieval audit: 2026-07-24

## Results used

The paper sharpens structural bounds for equality words of two non-periodic binary
morphisms with unique overflows. Its introduction records regularity of ternary equality
sets as a long-standing open problem, corroborating the structural discontinuity between
two and three source letters.

This is contextual evidence, not a current-status certificate for `GPCP(3)`: ordinary
homogeneous equality sets and fixed-boundary GPCP are distinct problems.
