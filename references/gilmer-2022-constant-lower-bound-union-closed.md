## Results used

Gilmer proves the first dimension-free positive bound: some element of every nontrivial
finite union-closed family occurs in at least one percent of its sets. The reusable engine is
the entropy-growth contrapositive: if all coordinate marginals are small and `A,B` are
independent copies, then `H(A ∪ B) > H(A)`, which is impossible when the support is
union-closed and `A` is uniform.

## Audit notes

The paper conjectures stronger scalar inequalities and a route toward `1/2`; later work proves
the golden-ratio inequality but disproves the proposed full-conjecture strengthening. We rely
on the entropy architecture, not on those conjectural endpoints.
