## Results used

Sawin sharpens Gilmer’s argument to `(3 − √5)/2` and introduces dependent sampling:
one mixes the independent coupling with a negatively dependent, coordinatewise coupling.
This is the conceptual source of Yu’s finite optimization and Cambie’s claimed
`0.382345533366703` endpoint. The paper also exhibits counterexamples to a stronger
Gilmer entropy conjecture.

## Audit notes

The strict improvement above `(3 − √5)/2` is sketched rather than reduced to an explicit
certified constant. Later numerical optimizations must therefore be audited independently.
