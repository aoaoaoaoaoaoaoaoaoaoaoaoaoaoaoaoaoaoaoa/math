## Results used

Theorem 1 proves the unconditional universal abundance bound
`(3 − √5)/2 ≈ 0.3819660112501051`. The proof turns Gilmer’s entropy-growth
criterion into a one-variable binary-entropy inequality and treats the extremal
probability measures by a concavity argument. This is the rigorous peer-reviewed
benchmark against which the present investigation measures improvement.

## Audit notes

The final analytic step is accompanied by numerical tables rather than a machine-checkable
certificate, but the sampled margins are large and the authors explain how interval bounds
would close the finite check. Boppana gives an independent calculus proof of the same binary
entropy inequality. Neither source proves a constant above `(3 − √5)/2`.
