## Result used

The paper gives explicit words `A,B,C` for Post's binary
deletion-width-three system such that every `AⁿBCᵐ` evolves to
`Aⁿ⁺¹BCᵐ⁺¹`. It therefore proves the existence of nonhalting,
nonperiodic, unbounded-growth configurations for that one system.

## Audit notes

The theorem does not decide reachability or halting for Post's system, prove
universality, or establish undecidability for the class of binary
deletion-width-three systems. It is evidence that the width-three boundary is
dynamically nontrivial, not the source theorem required by the scheduled
compiler.
