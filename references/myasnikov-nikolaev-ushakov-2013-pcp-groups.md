## Results used

The paper formulates PCP as an equalizer problem in groups and group GPCP as a double
twisted-conjugacy problem. It proves group-theoretic undecidability results in groups
containing nonabelian free subgroups.

These results cannot be read as classical `GPCP(3)`: group words admit inverses and free
reduction, and the rank parameter counts group generators under different semantics. The
paper is useful chiefly for locating the algebraic bridge that Carvalho later makes
algorithmically sharp.

## Audit notes

The local file is arXiv v2. The journal version uses “non-homogeneous PCP” for the
group analogue called GPCP in the preprint; neither convention changes the mismatch with
positive free-monoid GPCP.
