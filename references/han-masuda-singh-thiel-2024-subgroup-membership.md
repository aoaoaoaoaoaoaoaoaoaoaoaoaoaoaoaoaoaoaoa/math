## Synopsis

For positive integers `u,v`, the paper studies the group generated by

```text
Lᵤ=[[1,0],[u,1]],       Rᵥ=[[1,v],[0,1]].
```

Theorem 1 recalls Esbelin and Gutan's criterion for `u=v=k≥2`: a matrix in the
specified ambient congruence group belongs to `⟨Lₖ,Rₖ⟩` exactly when at least one
of `c/a` and `b/d` has a continued-fraction expansion whose partial quotients all
belong to `kℤ`.

Theorem 3 covers every `u,v≥1` with `u+v>4`. Applied to the short continued
fraction of `b/d`, the explicit transformation `fᵤ,ᵥ` decides whether the result
has the alternating `(u,v)` divisibility property. The proof also recovers the
exponents in the alternating product when membership holds. The paper records
that `⟨Lᵤ,Rᵥ⟩` is free when `u,v≥2`.

These are complete-matrix membership theorems. They do not decide whether some
matrix in the group maps one projective point to another.

## Source Assessment

Lemma 2 corrects Lemma 3.5 of the authors' earlier paper: the terminal factor is
`(−1)^β(γ+1)`, not `−(γ+1)`. The earlier sign error does not invalidate their
alternative proof of Sanov's theorem, but it can return word exponents with the
wrong sign. The 2024 criterion is the version used here.
