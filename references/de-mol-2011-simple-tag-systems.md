## Results used

The survey records that all then-known fixed-deletion-width universal tag
constructions used large alphabets. It treats Post's binary deletion-width-three
system as unresolved and analyzes its behavior experimentally. This supplies the
closest class-level boundary to the scheduled compiler's missing fixed-width
source.

## Audit notes

This source predates Neary's 2015 binary universality theorem. Neary fixes the
alphabet but makes the deletion width depend on the simulated cyclic program,
so the two results do not close the binary fixed-width-three seam.
