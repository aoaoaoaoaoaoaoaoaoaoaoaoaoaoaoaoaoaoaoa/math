## Results used

The paper proves decidability of GPCP when both morphisms are marked: distinct source
letters have images beginning in distinct target letters under each morphism. It derives a
shorter binary-PCP decidability proof as a consequence.

This is a structural obstruction for `GPCP(3)`. A source construction that becomes marked
after recoding has entered a decidable class, even if its role count is three.
