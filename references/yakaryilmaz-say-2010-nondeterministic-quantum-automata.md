## Results used

The paper proves that languages recognized by nondeterministic quantum finite automata
coincide with the exclusive stochastic languages. The acceptance semantics distinguish zero
from positive probability, making the work relevant to zero-language rather than
exact-series compression.

Physical quantum evolution, measurements, complex amplitudes, and squared norms are not
an integer `3 × 3` scalar-zero representation. Any use in `M₃(4)` must first extract a raw
generalized-finite-automaton amplitude with a sound integer realization.
