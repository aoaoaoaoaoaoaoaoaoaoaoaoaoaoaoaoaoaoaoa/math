## Results used

This survey explains the marked-morphism decidability arguments for PCP and GPCP and
their relation to the binary-source PCP proof. It also distinguishes these restrictions from
other PCP modifications.

For the present program, marked GPCP is a decidable stratum. A proposed undecidability
construction must preserve the unmarked prefix ambiguity on which the hard instances rely;
merely assigning distinct initial target letters to the three source images moves the problem
into established decidable machinery.
