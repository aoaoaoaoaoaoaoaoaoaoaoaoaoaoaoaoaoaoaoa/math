## Results used

Bouchard derives necessary conditions for a cardinality-minimal lattice counterexample.
Among them: the bottom is meet-reducible, the top is join-reducible, no meet-irreducible lies
below a join-irreducible, there is at most one doubly irreducible element, every nontrivial
element has at least three incomparable elements, and each meet-irreducible dominates a
join-irreducible whose filter has exactly `(|L|+1)/2` elements.
