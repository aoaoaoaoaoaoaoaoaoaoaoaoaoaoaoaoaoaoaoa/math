## Results used

This is the comprehensive pre-entropy survey. It supplies exact equivalences among
union-closed families, intersection-closed families, and finite lattices: every finite nontrivial
lattice should contain a join-irreducible `j` with `|↑j|≤|L|/2`. It also consolidates the
minimal-counterexample, smallest-set, FC-family, injection, and lower-semimodular results
needed to recognize rediscoveries and known dead ends.
