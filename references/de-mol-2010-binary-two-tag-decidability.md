## Results used

Theorem 1 decides reachability for every tag system with two symbols and
deletion width two; Corollary 1 decides halting. The discussion identifies the
adjacent binary deletion-width-three and ternary deletion-width-two classes as
the unresolved boundary and explains why the table method does not extend
directly.

## Audit notes

The paper follows Post's append-then-delete convention and warns that the
delete-then-append convention requires minor adjustments. Its notation
`TS(v,μ)` places deletion width before alphabet size, but one sentence in the
discussion appears to swap the two adjacent class labels. This project therefore
uses the unambiguous phrase “binary deletion-width-three.”
