## Results used

The paper decides PCP and computes equalizers for marked free-monoid morphisms and
immersions of free groups. It supplies an algebraic model for the structured, decidable side
of the equalizer problem and clarifies which free-group cancellation mechanisms are already
controlled.

For this campaign the negative lesson is exact: markedness or immersion structure is not a
route to `GPCP(3)` undecidability. The paper concerns homogeneous equality sets; fixed
boundaries require a separate GPCP argument.

## Audit notes

This is the peer-reviewed LIPIcs version. Its introduction described free-group PCP as open
in 2020; Carvalho's 2026 preprint supersedes that historical status.
