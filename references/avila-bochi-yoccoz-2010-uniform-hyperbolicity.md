## Synopsis

The paper characterizes uniform exponential growth for finite families of real unimodular
`2 × 2` matrices by their action on the projective line. For a full shift, Theorem 2.2 states
that uniform hyperbolicity is equivalent to a nonempty proper open multicone with finitely many
components whose image under every generator is compactly contained in the multicone. Theorem
2.3 gives the Markovian version: each automaton state has its own multicone, and every allowed
transition maps one strictly inside the next.

The paper also studies connected components and their boundary. Theorem 4.1 says that a boundary
point of a fixed hyperbolic component has a bounded witness of one of three kinds: a periodic
product equal to `±I`, a parabolic periodic product, or a heteroclinic incidence carrying the
unstable direction of one periodic product to the stable direction of another. Corollary 4.5
deduces that every connected hyperbolic component is semialgebraic. Sections 3 and 5 classify
the full two-shift more explicitly and show that higher-symbol combinatorics are more intricate.

The hypotheses are a finite family in `SL₂(ℝ)` and a finite-type language. The results do not
directly decide hyperplane return for nonunimodular matrices, a countably parametrized generator
family, or a three-dimensional exterior action.

## Source Assessment

The journal publication and arXiv v1 have the same title and theorem architecture; pagination
differs. No correction or retraction was found. The multicone characterization is qualitative;
the explicit decision procedure in Remark 3.14 is for pairs under the full two-shift and should
not be generalized to arbitrary finite families without proof.

## Project Use
