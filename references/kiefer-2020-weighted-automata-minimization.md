## Results used

The notes give a concise linear-algebraic proof that the Hankel rank of a series over a
field equals the minimum number of states in an exact weighted-automaton realization.
Reachability and observability yield the corresponding minimization algorithm.

This is the theorem behind the exact rank-four obstruction for the paired compiler. It
preserves every coefficient. It yields no lower bound for another rational series with the
same zero set.
