## Results used

The paper proves decidability of the Skolem problem for algebraic linear recurrence
sequences of order at most four. The proposed `M₃(2)` rank census needs only the older
order-three case to decide whether a scalar sequence `rAⁿc` ever vanishes, but this paper
supplies the current definitive low-order boundary and removes any field-specific ambiguity.

## Audit notes

This result concerns one scalar recurrence. It does not decide mortality of an arbitrary
matrix-valued recurrence family, nor the variable-index return problem
`{VAⁿU : n ≥ 0}` isolated by the `M₃(2)` attack.
