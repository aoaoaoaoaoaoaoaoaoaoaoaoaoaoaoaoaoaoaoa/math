## Synopsis

Theorem 3.1 decides Submonoid Membership in `Y⋊ℤⁿ` when `Y` is a finitely presented module over
`𝔽ₚ[X₁±,…,Xₙ±]`; this includes every prime-coefficient n-dimensional lamplighter group.
The reduction passes through products of conjugate subgroups and group knapsack. Theorem 3.3
proves that solution sets of the resulting module S-unit equations are effectively p-automatic,
and Theorem 3.4 gives the same conclusion for knapsack solution sets. Example 4.8 shows that
these automatic sets need not be semilinear.

Finite characteristic is essential to the proof: Frobenius powers and automaticity over one
prime field drive the S-unit theorem. The paper states that the infinite-coefficient lamplighter
case has undecidable Submonoid Membership and that even composite finite coefficients raise a
multi-base automatic-intersection problem. It also distinguishes Submonoid Membership from
Rational Subset Membership, which can already be undecidable in two-dimensional lamplighter
groups covered by the submonoid theorem.

## Project Use

Theorem 3.1 does not apply to the characteristic-zero cyclic module `ℤ[1/6]`, and the paper's
p-automatic solution sets do not justify a Presburger or semilinear model. It locates the missing
ingredient in the proposed `Γ₆` attack: synchronized characteristic-zero `2`/`3` arithmetic,
not the mere presence of a two-dimensional cursor.
