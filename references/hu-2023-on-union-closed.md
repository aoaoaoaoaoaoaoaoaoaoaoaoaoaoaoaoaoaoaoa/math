## Results used

Hu gives a new proof of the Lo Faro/Roberts–Simpson lower bound on the size of a
set-minimal counterexample and studies the former `ε`-version and the minimal maximal
frequency function. It is used to separate classical minimal-family restrictions from the newer
minimal-lattice attack.

## Audit notes

The `ε`-conjecture discussed in the manuscript was subsequently resolved by Gilmer. The paper
does not improve the modern dimension-free constant. Its introduction also records earlier
claimed proofs as false, a useful warning in this unusually error-prone literature.
