## Results used

This post-breakthrough survey maps the Gilmer, Sawin, Chase–Lovett, Alweiss–Huang–Sellke,
Yu, and Liu entropy routes. Its most useful negative information is structural: unrestricted
mixing loses its latent entropy after conditioning in the chain rule, and approximate
union-closed examples block the naive independent-coupling route.
