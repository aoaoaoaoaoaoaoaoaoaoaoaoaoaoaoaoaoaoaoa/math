## Results used

The paper studies vector and homing-vector automata, including models over `2 × 2`
integer matrices. It proves that a rational two-state generalized finite automaton can
separate any prescribed pair of distinct words.

This demonstrates that zero/nonzero behavior may be much more state-succinct than an
exact numerical series. Pairwise finite separation does not imply recognition of the
infinite terminal-match zero language, and homing acceptance is not automatically a single
integer coefficient test.
