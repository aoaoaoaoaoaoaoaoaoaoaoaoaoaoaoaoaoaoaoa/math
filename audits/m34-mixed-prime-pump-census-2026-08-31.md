## Free Macro Address

In the unnormalized integral chart, the two pumps are

```text
DT = [[6,10],[0,15]],       TD = [[6,15],[0,15]].
```

They induce `z↦(2/5)z+2/3` and `z↦(2/5)z+1`. For an address
`ε₁…εₙ`, put `dᵢ=2` for `DT` and `dᵢ=3` for `TD`. Its product has diagonal
`(6ⁿ,15ⁿ)` and upper entry

```text
u(ε₁…εₙ) = 5·3^(n-1) · Σᵢ dᵢ 2^(i-1)5^(n-i).
```

Equivalently, appending a macro with upper entry `b∈{10,15}` obeys

```text
u(wS)=15u(w)+b6^|w|.
```

Subtract two equal-length codes. Reduction modulo two forces the first digit difference to be
zero; dividing by two and repeating decodes the whole address from the left. Reduction modulo
five forces the last difference to be zero and peels from the right. Lean formalizes both residue
laws, proves integer-code injectivity at fixed length, realizes the code as the exact affine
offset. The multiplier first recovers the address length; the radix code then proves injectivity
of the expanded raw-word actions.

For a pure pump power, the upper entry is

```text
b(15ᵏ-6ᵏ)/9.
```

The two power offsets differ by `5(15ᵏ-6ᵏ)/9`; after affine normalization their reduced
denominator has `5`-adic valuation `-(k-1)` for `k≥1`. Pump depth is therefore visible in the
radix denominator as well as in the common multiplier.

This does not contradict the length-`27` and longer relations over raw `{D,T}` words. Freeness is
for the derived two-letter macro generators `{DT,TD}` and for equal macro length.

## Reader Boundary

For each schema, the contextual difference

```text
Φ(M)=A M C-B M D
```

is linear in the inserted `2×2` matrix. It vanishes on `I` and the chosen pump `S`, hence on every
`Sᵏ`. The certificate substitutes the alternate pump at the same cuts. All `23` exact offset
discrepancies are nonzero, so `Φ` is nonzero on the upper-entry direction.

Equivalently, the formal group normal form says that the connector `P=A⁻¹B` centralizes the chosen
pump and none of the alternate pumps.

At fixed length `n`, all `{DT,TD}` address matrices lie on one affine line with common diagonal.
Consequently `Φ(M_w)=0` exactly when `M_w=Sⁿ`. Formal fixed-length offset injectivity then gives
`w=[S,…,S]`. Each relation is an exact unary constant-address reader.

This is not an arbitrary equality reader. No certified context compares two independent binary
addresses, and no schema accepts both macro generators. Combining several unary readers does not
by itself make the source computation undecidable.
