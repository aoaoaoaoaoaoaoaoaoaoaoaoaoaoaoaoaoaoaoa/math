## Joint Adjudication

### Paired construction became singular and history-sensitive

The reset report kills every faithful residual-local reverse-queue chart whose two legal prepend
cylinders span three-space. The collision occurs entirely on legal input; an illegal-state trap or
denominator growth outside the chart does not repair it. A constructive survivor must collapse at
least one complete legal cylinder into a plane and spend the third coordinate transiently to
remember the route into that collapsed persistent state.

This condition cross-pollinates the fixed `bcbc` recognizer without promoting its fitted constants.
The useful feature is its transient guard. Uniformity remains absolute: all matrices and boundaries
must be computable from `(β,body)` on mortal, immortal, and null-history instances, and the theorem
must cover every control word.

### The paired lower bound lost its generic bridge

Positive common shifts, full reachability and observability, minimality, and even an injective
semantic embedding into a group do not imply backward cancellation. The explicit countermodel
prevents any future proof from invoking inverse saturation abstractly. A valid lower bound must
extract backward residual cancellativity from the paired terminal grammar itself and prove that
the required inverse orbit has cofinal positive representatives, or replace saturation with a new
broad shift-equivariant contradiction.

The constructive and lower-bound leaves remain a genuine race: each attacks the same paired
interface from opposite sides, but neither report made the other redundant.

### Global recoding must retain nonabelian computational residue

Finite-control exact side transport pumps to a stationary return and is impossible on four
explicit Neary pulses. A computable accepting-prefix residual bound also yields finite search.
The global report goes further: a finite union of capped periodic rays is one-counter territory,
even when only one computably selected witness per yes-instance has that normal form and the
arbitrary-word converse remains wholly global.

A surviving recoding must therefore expose at least two independent unbounded factors, genuine
word-order entropy at fixed length, or a halting-dependent normal form unavailable to the
reduction. State-dependent spelling and an indefinitely open single periodic carrier do not meet
this threshold.

### The native source acquired an exact interface

For productions `αₓX⟶Xβₓ`, the endpoint identity `sβ(w)=α(w)t` is always necessary. It is
sufficient exactly when it forces every cumulative `α` prefix. Under that hypothesis, three
productions compile literally to three GPCP pairs with boundaries `(s,ε,ε,t)`.

The unrestricted telescope underflows. The known three-rule semi-Thue theorem rewrites arbitrary
substrings, so a rule-name trace omits redex positions and contexts. Its cancellation rule may be
raw material for a word-valued context channel, but the external report's unverified exact source
transcription was not promoted.

One-sided corrected positive drift supplies the complementary obstruction. A finite-phase source
whose recurrent transitions drift in one direction under some positive weighting has bounded
successful configurations and decidable reachability. A universal source must recurrently expand
and contract under every positive weighting.

### Free cancellation no longer needs syntax normalization

Finite cancellative spelling state pumps the triangle identity words. Making the private third
coordinate singular is worse when two persistent coordinates retain an invertible cancellation
quotient: after the singular prefix every later quotient identity is exactly absorbed. A separate
same-zero normal-form filter is impossible below six states.

The retained Carvalho source supplies a different target. Its `p`-exponent is invariant through
the simulation, the constructed halting loop has exponent one, and any exponent-one fixed point
is nontrivial. The equalizer reduction therefore gives

```text
C halts  ↔  ∃u, g(u)=h(u) and κ(u)=1.
```

This target may identify every positive identity padding. It needs no chosen irreducible spelling
and evades the boundary-square collapse because `κ(u^n)=n`. Its natural state is already a
noncommutative discrepancy plus an integer counter, which is exactly the kind of two-factor
residue left open by the global one-counter obstruction.

## Five Raceable Leaves

### 1. History-sensitive singular paired constructor

Construct one source-computable rational family `λ,γ,H_b,H_c,H_t` for every admissible


for every control word. At least one legal prepend cylinder must lie in a plane, and a transient

per-instance synthesis do not count.

### 2. Paired residual saturation lower bound

Prove backward cancellativity and inverse-orbit cofinality from the complete paired terminal
grammar, then apply the checked `F₂×F₂` projective obstruction; or find another broad common-shift
contradiction. The proof must use paired-specific structure which the positive countermodel lacks.

### 3. Global word-residual GPCP recoding

Give a computable three-pair transformation of the verified four-role family with both witness
directions and a complete arbitrary-new-word converse. Its normal witness must retain two
unbounded factors, nonperiodic word order, or an unavailable halting-dependent description. Exact
side transport and finite-state parsing are forbidden ancestors.

### 4. Endpoint-forcing native three-schema source

Construct an undecidable family of exactly three prefix-normal productions satisfying endpoint
prefix forcing for every arbitrary trace. Its recurrent dynamics must have both drift signs under
every positive symbol weighting and an unbounded word-valued context channel. Once these source
claims hold, the checked compiler finishes `GPCP(3)`.

### 5. Exponent-one positive cancellation

Compile Carvalho's predicate `g(u)=h(u), κ(u)=1` through three positive source controls, either as
`GPCP(3)` or as a three-state scalar-zero family. Identity padding may be accepted. A survivor
needs an everywhere-invertible infinite fibre, an unbounded cocycle transporting `κ`, or semantics
which abandon the two-dimensional invertible quotient.

## Cross-Pollination

normal form may encode exactly that semidirect product. Leaves 3 and 4 share the endpoint-forcing

constructor is allowed to make the persistent semantics singular; the free-cancellation no-go

Leaf 2 remains an opposing theorem lane rather than a construction vocabulary. Any proposed

proposed lower bound should be tested against the checked positive-shift countermodel before
abstract generalization.
