# M₃(2) Cubic Null-Conic Orbit Audit

Date: 2026-08-10

Auditor and formalizer: GPT-5.6 Sol

Human role: elicited by @eternalism_4eva

## Enemy

The non-pure irreducible-cubic fork had already been reduced to finitely many singular endpoint
lines joined by words over an order-three recurrence of projective involutions. Two possible
collapses remained implicit: the rank-(3,2) origin might constrain the fixed twist, or the cubic
recurrence and involutivity might force finitely many bridge states or a bounded bridge length.

Both fail. Every faithful cubic family has a canonical trace model with an arbitrary left twist,
and one explicit physical family contains a free binary submonoid with an injective rational-line
orbit. The remaining problem is endpoint-faithful reachability on a rational null conic.

## Canonical Trace Model

Let `K/ℚ` be the cubic field generated by the ambient action, `τ` its trace, and

```text
K₀=ker τ,    π(x)=x−τ(x)/3.
```

The trace pairing `g(u,v)=τ(uv)` is nondegenerate on `K₀`. For `x∈K`, define

```text
Tₓ(u)=π(xu),    u∈K₀.
```

In a basis `e₁,e₂` of `K₀`, put `G=(τ(eᵢeⱼ))` and `Hₓ=(τ(xeᵢeⱼ))`; then
`Tₓ=G⁻¹Hₓ`. The map from `K` to the three-dimensional space of `g`-self-adjoint endomorphisms
of `K₀` is an isomorphism. Injectivity is immediate: `Hₓ=0` would put the two-dimensional space
`xK₀` inside `K₀⊥=ℚ` for nonzero `x`.

Every rational plane in `K` is `a⁻¹K₀` for some `a∈K×`. Applying this to the input-column plane
and the trace-dual output-row plane gives, after one rational interface conjugation,

```text
Cₙ=F Tₓₙ,    xₙ=γθⁿ,
```

where `θ` represents the ambient action, `γ∈K×`, and `F∈GL₂(ℚ)`. Conversely, given
`(K,θ,γ,F)`, taking the input columns to be a basis of `K₀` and choosing the output trace-dual
vectors from `FG⁻¹` realizes this family. The twist `F` is arbitrary; rank-(3,2) contributes no
hidden compatibility law.

The determinant and singular geometry are explicit:

```text
d(x)=det Tₓ=(τ(x)²−τ(x²))/6=N(x)τ(x⁻¹)/3,
Tₓ singular ⇔ x⁻¹∈K₀,
ker Tₓ=⟨x⁻¹⟩,    im Tₓ=⟨x⁻¹⟩⊥.
```

Thus the determinant-zero conic is parametrized by field inversion on `ℙ(K₀)`, and the singular
indices are exactly the zeros of `τ(γ⁻¹θ⁻ⁿ)`. This is the earlier order-three singularity
recurrence in canonical coordinates, not a new branch.

## Reflection And Null-Conic Forms

Choose a nonzero alternating form `Ω` on `K₀`, put `S=G⁻¹Ω`, and define `Jₓ=STₓ`. Then
`J:K→sl₂(ℚ)` is a linear isomorphism and

```text
Jₓ²=q(x)I,    q(x)=−det Jₓ.
```

At a singular point, `im Jₓ=ker Jₓ=⟨x⁻¹⟩`. Writing `Q=FS⁻¹` recovers the existing common-left
reflection form `Cₙ=QJₓₙ`; the preceding converse proves that `Q` is arbitrary.

The symmetric product remains in the trace pencil,

```text
(TₓTᵧ+TᵧTₓ)/2=T_(x∘y),
x∘y=(τ(x)y+τ(y)x)/3+(τ(xy)−τ(x)τ(y))1/6,
```

but ordinary multiplication adds the one missing skew coordinate. Equivalently, with `B` the
polar form of `q` and `Jₓ×ᵧ=[Jₓ,Jᵧ]/2`,

```text
JₓJᵧ=B(x,y)I+J_(x×y),
(a,x)(b,y)=(ab+B(x,y), ay+bx+x×y).
```

This is the full split-quaternion algebra `M₂(ℚ)`. Distinct recurrence reflections already
generate the missing coordinate, so trace/Jordan closure supplies no smaller multiplicative
state space.

Conjugation by the arbitrary `Q` induces its rational adjoint isometry `ρ=Ad_Q∈O(q)(ℚ)`. For
unit indices,

```text
(Jₓ₁Q)⋯(JₓₖQ)=Jₓ₁Jρx₂⋯Jρ^(k−1)xₖ Qᵏ.
```

Conjugation by `Jₓ` acts on `K` as the orthogonal involution

```text
rₓ(y)=2B(x,y)x/q(x)−y.
```

Consequently the bridge between singular indices `s,t` exists exactly when

```text
[(rₓₙ₁ρ)⋯(rₓₙₖρ)x_t]=[ρ⁻¹x_s]
```

on the null conic `q=0`, with independently selected nonsingular indices `nᵢ`. Scanning a zero
word between consecutive singular occurrences proves that these finitely many ordered endpoint
pairs contain every mortality witness. Finite singular timing does not bound the unit bridge.

## Free Physical Bridge

Take

```text
A=[[0,0,1],[1,0,1],[0,1,0]],
U=[[0,−2/3],[1,0],[0,1]],
V=[[0,0,31],[1,0,0]].
```

Direct rational multiplication gives

```text
A³=A+I,                  χ_A(X)=X³−X−1,
M₀=VU=[[0,31],[0,−2/3]],
M₁=VAU=[[31,0],[0,1]],
M₂=VA²U=[[0,31/3],[1,0]],
M₄=VA⁴U=[[31,31/3],[1,1]].
```

The cubic is irreducible by the rational-root test and is non-pure. Explicit one-sided inverses
certify that `U,V` have rank two:

```text
[[0,1,0],[0,0,1]]U=I,
V[[0,1],[0,0],[1/31,0]]=I.
```

Also `det A=1`, and `M₀,M₁,M₂` are linearly independent. Hence this
is a faithful physical irreducible-cubic family, not an abstract `PGL₂` example.

Put

```text
F=M₁=[[31,0],[0,1]],    R=[[1,1/3],[1,1]],    M₄=FR.
```

On `ℙ¹(ℝ)`, `F(t)=31t` and `R(t)=(t+1/3)/(t+1)`. Let `a=1/√3` and
`w(t)=(t−a)/(t+a)`; then `w(Rt)=(2−√3)w(t)`. The four ping-pong domains

```text
X_F⁻={|t|<1/10},
X_F⁺={|t|>3}∪{∞},
X_R⁺=(√3/12,4√3/3),
X_R⁻=(−4√3/3,−√3/12)
```

are pairwise disjoint. The inequalities

```text
F(ℙ¹∖X_F⁻)⊂X_F⁺,      F⁻¹(ℙ¹∖X_F⁺)⊂X_F⁻,
R(ℙ¹∖X_R⁻)⊂X_R⁺,      R⁻¹(ℙ¹∖X_R⁺)⊂X_R⁻
```

follow from `31/10>3`, `3/31<1/10`, and `2−√3<(3/5)²`. Ping-pong proves
`⟨F,R⟩≅⟨F⟩*⟨R⟩`. Encoding binary letters by `0↦F`, `1↦FR` gives a unique free-product normal
form, so the physical unit returns `M₁,M₄` generate a free binary submonoid in `PGL₂(ℚ)`.

The rational point `5/2` lies outside all four domains. Every nonidentity reduced word moves it
into a ping-pong domain, so its rational-line orbit is injective. Cubic recurrence order,
reflection identities, and finite singular timing therefore do not by themselves make the
projective bridge set finite or bound bridge length.

This does not prove undecidability. The chosen binary subgroup has an effective normal form, and
`5/2` is not asserted to be a singular endpoint line. Any closure must exploit the special
endpoint placement omitted by the free-orbit witness.
