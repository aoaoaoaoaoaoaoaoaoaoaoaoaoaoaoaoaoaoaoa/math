## Verdict

Bounded primitive-height recurrence along a free normal-form inverse path is
always a target-stabilizer phenomenon. Let a group `G` act on `P¹(ℚ)`, fix a
target `q`, and let `g₀,g₁,…` be pairwise distinct elements. If each `gᵢq`
has a coprime integral representative `(aᵢ,bᵢ)` satisfying

```text
max(|aᵢ|,|bᵢ|) ≤ H,
```

then two representatives coincide. For the corresponding distinct prefixes,

```text
gᵢq = gⱼq,       (gⱼ⁻¹gᵢ)q = q,       gⱼ⁻¹gᵢ ≠ 1.
```

Hence `Stab_G(q)` is nontrivial. Contrapositively, trivial target stabilizer
forces the primitive heights along every injective-prefix orbit to be
unbounded.

This removes the proposed bounded recurrent successor to `D2-O05`. A harder
promised-empty instance with a free normal form and trivial target stabilizer
cannot remain forever inside a bounded primitive-height box. It must escape to
unbounded height or fail a different effective step, such as target-stabilizer
recognition.

## Formal Statement

`InverseOrbitRecurrence.lean` defines

```text
PrimitivePair = {(a,b) : ℤ² // IsCoprime a b},
primitiveHeight(a,b) = max(|a|,|b|),
primitivePoint(a,b) = [a:b] ∈ P¹(ℚ).
```

It proves:

1. `integralPairBox_finite`: the integral box of height at most `H` is finite.
2. `primitivePairBox_finite`: its coprime-pair subtype is finite.
3. `exists_nontrivial_stabilizer_of_orbit_collision`: equality
   `gᵢq=gⱼq` with `gᵢ≠gⱼ` yields the nonidentity stabilizer element
   `gⱼ⁻¹gᵢ`.
4. `exists_nontrivial_stabilizer_of_bounded_primitive_orbit`: an infinite
   injective sequence of group elements with bounded primitive orbit
   representatives yields such a collision and stabilizer.
5. `primitiveHeight_unbounded_of_stabilizer_trivial`: a trivial target
   stabilizer forces an orbit representative above every height bound.

The collision theorem transports a repeated intermediate projective ray back
to the original target; no faithfulness assumption on the action is needed.
For the intended free-product application, faithfulness supplies injectivity
of reduced normal-form prefixes.

## Relation To `D2-O05`

The explicit `D2-O05` parser follows

```text
[3:5] --U⁻¹→ [3:1] --D⁻¹→ [3:5]
height:          5  →  3  →  5.
```

The positive word `UD` fixes `[3:5]`. `D2-O06` shows that this agreement is
structural: every bounded recurrence generated by distinct group prefixes
produces a nontrivial target stabilizer by taking their quotient. There is no
second class of bounded-height free-orbit recurrence waiting after the
stabilizer component is removed.

The result does not make the target-stabilizer test effective. It separates
the frontier into two obligations:

```text
bounded recurrence  →  recognize or quotient the target stabilizer;
trivial stabilizer  →  control an unbounded-height inverse path.
```

## Scope

The theorem does not prove termination, decidability, or undecidability of
`UCB₂(S)`. It supplies no rate of height growth and no computable search bound.
An unbounded path may revisit smaller heights infinitely often. The supplied
primitive pairs need not be canonical up to sign; boundedness still leaves
only finitely many signed pairs, which is sufficient for the pigeonhole
argument.

The prefix sequence must be injective as group elements. An unreduced syntax
stream may revisit one group element without producing a nonidentity quotient;
it must first be reduced to a faithful normal form. No claim is made for paths
whose group prefixes are not distinct.
