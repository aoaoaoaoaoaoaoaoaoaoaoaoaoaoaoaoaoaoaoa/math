## Scope

This audit separates proved universal bounds, numerical candidate bounds, structural class
theorems, and unproved reductions relevant to Frankl's union-closed sets conjecture. It is
based on complete inspection of the retained sources and of the metadata-only sources whose
inspected digests appear in their sidecars. The local bibliography records the exact versions,
licenses, hashes, and source-specific defects.

The conjecture remained open in the April 2026 literature. In its set form, every finite
nontrivial union-closed family has a coordinate occurring in at least half its members. In its
lattice form, every finite nontrivial lattice has a join-irreducible `j` with
`|↑j|≤|L|/2`. For an intersection-closed family, the dual conclusion is a coordinate omitted
from at least half its members.

## Entropy Line

Gilmer's durable mechanism samples independent uniform members `A,B` and contradicts
union closure by proving `H(A∪B)>H(A)`. Sawin determines the sharp independent-coupling
constant and introduces a second, negatively dependent sample. Yu places this in a maximal-
correlation optimization and proves that Sawin's mixture may be tested on symmetric couplings
of the form

```text
(1−β)Q_(a₁,a₂)+βQ_(b₁,b₂).
```

Yu's printed proof applies a fixed-mean concavity lemma as if it were global. The gap is
repairable by raising every marginal of mean below `t` to exact mean `t` with mass at
`(1,1)` before taking extreme points. The repaired reduction is analytic; the source's
numerical minimization remains uncertified. The obstruction at the claimed optimum is explicit:
a marginal supported on `{b,1}`, where `b≈0.3294547385030369723917053839`, defeats every
mixture of the independent and max-entropy protocols above
`c*=0.38234553336670272114599300…`. The defining entropy equation, exact factorization, and
rigorous root enclosure are recorded in the
[affine-wall audit](frankl-affine-wall-2026-08-10.md).

The local campaign reduced the repaired program to two bivariate families, first certified
`19099/50000`, then `76469/200000`, and finally
`38234553336670271/10^17`. The finite entropy bridge,
functional reductions, analytic regions, and low-rectangle certificate are now Lean-checked;
see the [formal result audit](frankl-rational-abundance-2026-08-10.md). The separate Arb run is
retained only as an outward-rounded regression oracle.

Liu's conditionally IID protocol escapes that obstruction because the extremal antithetic
coupling is not a mixture of IID laws. His analytic compactness argument proves a strict gain.
For the displayed `0.382709087…` candidate, however, cardinality reduction uses concavity of
the kernel generated by `f(x)=x(1−x)`. The required infinite coefficient matrix is only checked
on grids and finite truncations. The ensuing nine-dimensional minimum is inferred from random-
start numerical optimization. These are independent proof obligations.

## Weighted And Geometric Lines

Karpas proves the conjecture for a union-closed family occupying at least half a Boolean cube.
Gendler extends the argument to product measure: sufficient weighted mass forces a coordinate
with the corresponding conditional majority. Karpas's directed-influence labels are reversed
in the preprint; the raw-edge reconstruction in the bidual-Horn audit repairs the proof, and
Gendler's convention independently corroborates the sign.

Zargar replaces Boolean labels by finite semigroup fibers and proves weighted half-frequency
theorems. His omitted binary case is now closed by the local kernel audit: every nontrivial
intersection-closed family has a coordinate omitted from strictly more than half the mass under
weights `2^(−|A|)`. The full-fiber semigroup audit also proves that the architecture cannot
reach homogeneous weight above `16/27`, and at uniform weight encounters the same golden-ratio
point-mass obstruction as the independent entropy method.

For the complement of a normalized union-closed family, Bhasin's cubical complex is acyclic;
the inspected induction actually proves contractibility. The downward cube boundary is exactly
the number of uniquely rooted vertices. Fourier expansion forces a strict lower bound on this
number in any counterexample, but contractibility alone supplies no contrary upper bound.

## Lattice And Horn Lines

Bouchard gives strong necessary conditions for a cardinality-minimal lattice counterexample:
irreducible deletion creates a local two-cover diamond; bottom and top have the opposite
reducibilities; meet-irreducibles never lie below join-irreducibles; and critical filters are
forced around meet-irreducibles. These constraints do not close the lattice argument.
Carvalho and Machiavelo provide the corresponding canonical root-deletion recursion for
normalized families, but no abundance quantity known from their paper is monotone under it.

Tian's 2022 preprint states the strict no-empty-set form of Frankl's conjecture for families
whose maximum chain has at most three members. Colbert's peer-reviewed 2026 theorem proves
non-strict abundance for the corresponding dimension-two posets, even for infinite families.
Thus the rank-three lattice exclusion rediscovered in the local counterexample audit is not a
new statement. Tian's strictness is what permits a deleted bottom member to be restored in
both parities; Colbert's non-strict theorem alone leaves the odd-cardinality equality case. The
local incidence count independently proves the full finite rank-three exclusion.

Lozin and Zamaraev state the bidual and self-dual Horn cases as open. The density dichotomy in
the local audit closes both: apply Karpas to the larger of the true family and the
set-complement of the false family. A maximal complement-free intersection-closed completion
need not be self-dual, so this class theorem does not reduce arbitrary Horn instances.
