## Research consequence

Use the exact fixed universal compiler image before quantifying over every admissible tag
body. Its deletion width is fixed, its bodies begin with `b`, and they have strict length
surplus and track structure. A counterexample outside that image refutes a proposed uniform
lemma, but does not by itself refute the intended reduction. Conversely, a successful finite
benchmark or cylinder theorem does not establish the arbitrary-code converse.

The next campaign should budget around one named missing implication per construction,
not around the number of available agents. Complete endpoints, source-image invariants, or
counterexamples to those exact implications count as progress; another bounded chamber with
no consuming theorem does not.
