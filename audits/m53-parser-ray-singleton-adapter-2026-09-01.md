## Extinction Boundary

A singleton target over one rule-ended root block is impossible without an ancestry assumption.
The root upper code, lift, marker, and final digit are decimal units, while the singleton trace
has shell `(β+1,β)`; the two valuation balances demand incompatible upper lengths.

For a deeper history, `AdmitsUnitPeeledCarrier` states the remaining premise exactly: the older
ray has nonzero numerator and admits peeled coordinates `N,D`, both in decimal shell `(0,0)`.
Under this premise, the existing recursive carrier theorems exclude a singleton current block.
If the current block obeys the parser's erasure boundary law, it must instead have at least two
roles and at least `β+3` upper digits.

The premise is not a theorem about all parser histories. Projective representability alone is
cheap; simultaneous decimal unitality of both peeled coordinates is the substantive ancestry
claim. Integral descent `D=gap·Nprev` and the factor support needed by the quotient gates are
strictly stronger and also remain unproved at this outer interface.
