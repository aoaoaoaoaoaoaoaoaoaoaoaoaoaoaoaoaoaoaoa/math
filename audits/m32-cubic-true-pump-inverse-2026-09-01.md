## Structural Fracture

The right bridge sends the common ray `(4,3)` to a nonzero multiple of `e₀`; its tail after one
wait does so as well. This is not an accident of the chosen numbers. Any connector architecture
whose complete right tail maps the common ray to terminal `e₀` has that tail among its own
suffixes, where the separator row annihilates it. Lean proves the resulting general failure of
the first-hit suffix-safety predicate for every surrounding head and terminal program.
