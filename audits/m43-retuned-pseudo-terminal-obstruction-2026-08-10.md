## Legal No-Instance

For width three and body `bbcc`, the initial queue is `ccb`, while the tag outputs are

```text
b ↦ b,    c ↦ bbccb.
```

Lean constructs the complete deterministic orbit

```text
ccb → bbccb → cbb → bbccb → ⋯
```

and applies the finite-halting descent theorem to this perpetually progressing three-state
invariant. Hence the queue never becomes shorter than three. The checked Neary source theorem
then excludes every genuine four-tile terminal match. The static promises are discharged in the
kernel: `2<3`, `3−1≤|bbcc|`, and `3−1∣|bbcc|`.

## Malformed Terminal

At width three, direct matrix evaluation proves that the gap-thirty `b` atom is the sparse
semantic matrix with upper word `10001` and lower word `11110`. It therefore behaves as the
extra tag production `b↦ccb` once its two erasers are included.

The formalized 33-atom reduced word uses this pseudo-production once. Its upper spelling followed
by the marker `1000` is definitionally equal to its lower spelling. The corresponding literal
physical context contains only the root and the two data generators and has length 100.

The existing common-image contraction and exceptional-blade factorization reduce that context
to the terminal bridge. Lean proves that it is a nonzero column times `r₃`; multiplication by an
arbitrary continuation is therefore zero exactly when that continuation annihilates `r₃`. No
cyclic rotation, intended-block assumption, or bounded word search enters.

Uniformly multiplying each rational generator by 24 only multiplies a length-`n` product by
`24ⁿ`. The obstruction therefore survives the same integral clearing as the proposed reduction,
although no duplicate integral family is retained.
