## Master Spine

The root implications are

```text
GPCP(3) undecidable
    ⇒ three-state scalar-zero undecidable
    ⇒ M₃(4) undecidable,
```

while a direct four-generator mortality construction can bypass GPCP. The generic scalar lift is
finished: three rational `3 × 3` controls with a source-uniform all-word same-zero theorem become
four rational mortality generators by adjoining one outer product, and independent denominator
clearing gives integer generators. No prompt should spend effort on separator placement or the
rational-to-integer step.

The square-root architecture is a second finished mortality grammar. One distinguished rank-two
matrix squares to the separator, so mortality is exactly scalar zero on the free-monoid subshift
avoiding two adjacent distinguished letters. It consumes one active semantic role and the
punctuation slot at once.

## Joint Adjudication

### Fixed branching is not the wall

The `bcbc` report supplied an exact regular grammar and rational singular recognizer. Its forward
residual recurrence was false; reconstruction replaced it with canonical paths and backward
entrance classification. Lean checks the grammar, recurrence, determinants, decoder, and every
intended zero. An audited finite reverse graph supplies the arbitrary-word converse.

Consequently `bcbc` cannot prove a four-state lower bound. Branching width alone is no obstacle:
one expanding carry plus a transient guard recognizes the complete fixed language. The valuable
mechanism is the transient guard, not the fitted constants. A universal compiler must produce its
parameters computably from every source body; `G3-O04` excludes a source-uniform finite-target
version of the same one-coordinate architecture.

### Punctuation is solved; its zero set is not

The direct mortality report found a source-uniform rank-two square root of the terminal outer
product. Formalization strengthened its grammar: `S²=uvᵀ` alone, over any field, is enough to
fracture every zero product to an `SS`-free scalar zero and conversely. No rank, intended placement,
or maximal-run hypothesis is needed.

Exact coefficient preservation on the `SS`-free subshift, even after nonzero letterwise scaling,
forces the semantic `R_b` matrix to be invertible. The displayed square root is singular. Hence
the remaining compiler must change nonzero values word-dependently. Additive rank-one fusion and
the reverse-marker family are audited dead ends.

### No cleverer fixed ternary macro remains

The GPCP report removed erasure as a loophole. The four Neary role pairs have four independent
paired-Parikh channels, so every exact fixed macro factorization uses at least four physical
letters even when either target morphism erases and codewords are empty, coincident, or
nonuniquely decoded. Lean checks the complete theorem.

The audited stationary extension allows arbitrary boundary fragments and output overlap, asking
only that a full rule-plus-deletion block return to one common residual. It is impossible on the
mixed universal bodies. Its arithmetic throat is Lean-checked. Thus the next Neary compiler must
change a role's spelling with state, share letters nonfactorially across role boundaries, keep a
residual open between blocks, or preserve only global solvability under a new target encoding.

Nicolas's two copy letters recur inside every derivation certificate; literal boundary absorption
cannot remove them. A distinct source route must either compile synchronization more tightly or
begin from a genuinely universal three-schema system.

### Free cancellation solves deletion, not spelling

The free-group report computed the closed-path rank `3m+1` but also showed why it is irrelevant:
the accepting fixed subgroup is trivial or cyclic, so Carvalho's equalizer is promised rank zero
or one. The cyclic generator depends on the halting history and cannot be chosen from a
computable finite menu.

Three positive letters already surject onto `F₂`; Lean checks this and the nonempty identity
relations. If a fixed homomorphic boundary test accepts an element and its square, it accepts a
nonempty positive identity spelling. This kills all-loop-complete quotient-blind compression, but
not an existential-only or spelling-sensitive compiler. The surviving task is to couple the
seven-state forbidden-triple normal form to Carvalho's program-dependent virtual endomorphism,
or realize the same one-way trap through singular matrices.

## Net Closed Territory

The following are no longer research nodes:

- exact rank-three realization, similarity, quotient, restriction, or nonzero letter scaling of
  the paired coefficient series;
- any fixed Neary role macro, with or without erasure, and any fixed-spelling overlap which closes
  to one residual at each complete block;
- source-uniform finite-mode expanding one-coordinate history with finitely many target fibers;
- support/minrank lower bounds which ignore the common shifts;
- inverse-saturated two-side projective dynamics in three dimensions;
- the fixed bodies `bcbb` and `bcbc` as state-dimension lower-bound candidates;
- separator placement, denominator clearing, and additive role/punctuation fusion;
- compression of Carvalho's complete Stallings basis or quotient-blind representation of all
  accepting group loops.

## Raceable Leaves

Seven leaves are mature enough for independent attacks. A result at any leaf must either close an
ancestor or kill the leaf at a scope that changes this list.

### Source-uniform paired constructor

Construct computable rational `λ,γ,H_b,H_c,H_t` from every checked `(β,body)` such that

```text
λH_wγ=0  ↔  pairedCoefficient(β,body,w)=0
```

for every control word. This immediately proves `M₃(4)`. It must use history beyond one expanding
affine coordinate and avoid inverse saturation. The fixed `bcbc` transient guard is a local
prototype, not a uniform solution.

### Positive-common-shift lower bound

Prove that no such three-state source-uniform paired constructor exists, ideally by deriving the
inverse saturation forbidden by `G3-O05` from positive all-context shift equivariance, or by an
equally broad decidability theorem for singular positive dynamics. This would kill the entire
checked paired route, not `M₃(4)` itself. Static zero support cannot carry the proof.

### Square-root `SS`-free compiler

Keep the checked punctuation square root and construct the other three matrices so that the old
paired zero set is recognized on every `SS`-free word after the role assignment. A broad no-go for
all zero-set representations on that subshift would kill the architecture. Exact values,
similarity, gauges, per-letter weights, and additive fusion are already closed.

### Stateful or open-residue Neary serialization

Compile the checked four-role Neary family to three source letters while retaining enough local
semantic structure to prove a complete arbitrary-word converse. At least one role spelling must
depend on state, two roles must share letters nonfactorially, or the residual must remain open
across deletion-width blocks. Returning to one fixed block residual invokes `G3-O08`.

### Global solvability-only GPCP recoding

Transform the checked four-role family to three GPCP pairs without preserving individual roles or
complete block outputs. The target words and fixed boundaries may implement a new noncommutative
normal form, but both witness transport and rejection of every new equality must be proved. This
is distinct from stateful serialization because local role recovery is abandoned entirely.

### Native three-schema computational source

Bypass Neary and Nicolas. Produce an undecidable rewriting, queue, tag, or transducer source whose
complete certificates use exactly three active morphism schemas, then compile it directly to
GPCP(3). Count schemas, not states, data symbols, or rules hidden in boundaries. One-rule
accessibility and literal absorption of Nicolas's recurrent copy alphabet are dead.

### Spelling-sensitive positive cancellation

Compile Carvalho's inverse-transducer discrepancy into three positive letters without first
forgetting the positive normal form. Legal words must realize free cancellation and the virtual
endomorphism; every reducible identity factor, partial reduction, cross-boundary forbidden triple,
and nonclosed path must enter a permanent nonaccepting residue. Either a GPCP(3) compiler or a
three-state scalar-zero realization closes the master.

## Nonleaves

Generic projective dynamics, exclusive stochastic automata, Rees or Brandt ideals, and singular
reset semigroups remain useful toolkits, but do not yet define independent research nodes. Each
needs a source interface and an all-word target theorem; until then it belongs inside the paired
or square-root construction prompts. Likewise, another fixed-body fit, another fixed macro, or
another punctuation placement would repeat closed territory.
