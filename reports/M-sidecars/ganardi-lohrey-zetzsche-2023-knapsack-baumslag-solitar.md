## Synopsis

Theorem 3.1 puts the power-word problem for every two-generator triangular group `T(α)` in
`TC⁰`. Theorem 4.1 proves that knapsack in `BS(1,q)=ℤ[1/q]⋊ℤ` is NP-complete. In contrast,
Theorem 5.1 makes systems of exponent equations undecidable even in `BS(1,q)`. The knapsack
proof uses existential Büchi arithmetic for one base `q`; the paper leaves knapsack in general
`T(α)` open.
