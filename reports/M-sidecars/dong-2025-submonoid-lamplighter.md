## Synopsis

Theorem 3.1 decides submonoid membership in `Y⋊ℤⁿ` whenever `Y` is a finitely presented module
over `𝔽ₚ[X₁±,…,Xₙ±]`. Theorem 3.3 makes the underlying module S-unit solution sets effectively
`p`-automatic; Theorem 3.4 does the same for knapsack solution sets. The result includes
`(ℤ/pℤ)≀ℤⁿ` in every dimension. It explicitly relies on finite characteristic and does not extend
to the infinite coefficient ring `ℤ`; it does not decide general rational-subset membership.
