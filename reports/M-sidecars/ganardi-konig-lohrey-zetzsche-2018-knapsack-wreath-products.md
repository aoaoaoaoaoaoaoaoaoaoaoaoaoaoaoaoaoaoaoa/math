## Synopsis

The paper studies knapsack equations in wreath products and proves closure of the class of
knapsack-semilinear groups under wreath products. Its terminal result makes knapsack and subset
sum NP-complete in `G≀ℤ` for every nontrivial finitely generated abelian group `G`, including
`ℤ≀ℤ`. This decidability coexists with the stronger submonoid-membership undecidability proved
by Lohrey, Steinberg, and Zetzsche.
