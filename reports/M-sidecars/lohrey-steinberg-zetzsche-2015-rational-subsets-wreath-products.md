## Synopsis

Theorem 3 decides rational-subset membership in `H≀V` when `H` is finite and `V` is virtually
free, using automaton saturation and a well-quasi-order. Theorem 6 constructs one fixed finitely
generated submonoid of `ℤ≀ℤ` with undecidable membership by simulating a fixed two-counter
machine. Therefore rational-subset membership in `ℤ≀ℤ` is undecidable as well.

The negative construction stores independent integer data at arbitrarily many cursor positions.
It does not transfer to finite-Prüfer-rank affine groups whose normal subgroup is a localization
of `ℤ`.
