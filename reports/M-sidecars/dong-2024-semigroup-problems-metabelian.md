## Synopsis

Theorem 1.1 decides the Group Problem, Identity Problem, and Inverse Problem uniformly for all
finitely generated metabelian groups given by finite metabelian presentations. The proof relates
positive words to labelled graphs and homogeneous equations over polynomial semirings. It does
not decide whether an arbitrary target belongs to a generated semigroup; the paper contrasts its
positive theorem with known semigroup-membership undecidability in some metabelian groups.
